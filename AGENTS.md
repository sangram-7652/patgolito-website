# PATGOLITO Brand Guidelines & System Rules

## 1. Official Brand Logo Rules (STRICT & MANDATORY)
- **Source of Truth**: The official PATGOLITO logo is the exact image from `https://i.ibb.co/VW95dCD8/final-yes-to-logo.jpg` (local assets: `/public/patgolito_logo.png`, `/public/patgolito_logo.jpg`, `/src/assets/patgolito_logo.png`, `/src/assets/patgolito_logo.jpg`).
- **Fixed Asset Rule**: The official PATGOLITO logo is a fixed, immutable brand asset. **NEVER** let AI regenerate, approximate, redraw, simplify, recolor, distort, stretch, rotate, or modify it.
- **Anatomy & Alignment**:
  - The first location pin sits **directly above the "P"**.
  - The second location pin sits **directly above the final "O"**.
  - The delivery truck travels along the curved route arrow connecting the first pin to the second pin.
  - The bold, modern "PATGOLITO" wordmark sits below.
- **NEVER** use old logo versions, orange branding, or substitute the logo with generic text or different icons.
- **Vehicle & Uniform Branding**: In all vehicle shots, fleet previews, and uniform assets, always use the clean, official PATGOLITO branding. Any vehicle manufacturer brand logos must remain hidden/removed.

## 2. STRICT COLOR PALETTE CONSTRAINT: NO BLUE COLOR ANYWHERE
- **DO NOT USE BLUE COLOR ANYWHERE ON THE SITE**.
- Primary palette: High-contrast Solid Black (`#000000`, `bg-black`, `text-black`), Pure/Off-White (`#FFFFFF`, `bg-white`, `text-white`), Sleek Charcoal / Zinc (`zinc-900`, `zinc-800`).
- Accent / Status palette: Emerald Green (`emerald-600`, `emerald-500`, `emerald-400`), Dark Charcoal, and Amber.
- Any blue classes (`bg-blue-*`, `text-blue-*`, `border-blue-*`, etc.) or orange branding are strictly forbidden.
