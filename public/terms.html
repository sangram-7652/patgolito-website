TERMS & CONDITIONS – PATGOLITO

Operating Entity: Zoago Logistics Solutions Private Limited • Brand: PATGOLITO • Jurisdiction: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Platform Service & Intermediary Model

Zoago Logistics Solutions Private Limited ('PATGOLITO') operates as a technology-enabled logistics platform connecting customers with independent vehicle owners and commercial drivers for transportation services. PATGOLITO facilitates booking, coordination, and route guidance between users and service providers. All transportation services are performed by independent verified drivers under the Carriage by Road Act, 2007.

2. Independent Drivers

Drivers registered on the PATGOLITO platform are independent commercial partners and not employees or agents of PATGOLITO. Drivers are required to maintain valid commercial driving licences, vehicle fitness certificates, commercial insurance, and all statutory permits under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

3. Customer Responsibility

Customers must provide accurate pickup and delivery details while placing orders. Customers are responsible for ensuring that goods are properly packed and suitable for transportation. Incorrect details or attempts to ship restricted commodities may result in cancellation or statutory penalties.

4. Upfront Pricing and Payments

Service charges are calculated using a calibrated upfront distance model based on vehicle base fare and calculated road kilometers. Fares shown before booking submission are final for the specified route; drivers are strictly prohibited from demanding cash bargaining or spot tips. Toll charges and parking fees, if incurred during transit, are payable as per actual receipts.

5. Loading Helpers & Labour Guidelines

Where helper services are selected during booking (+₹300 flat per helper), helpers assist in loading and unloading goods between the vehicle and destination ground/elevator access. Customers are advised to secure fragile, antique, or delicate items appropriately.

6. Prohibited and Hazardous Goods

The carriage of hazardous chemicals, flammable substances, explosives, firearms, contraband, or illegal narcotics under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985 is strictly prohibited. PATGOLITO and driver partners reserve the right to refuse or terminate transport of suspected contraband immediately.

7. Governing Law and Exclusive Jurisdiction

These Terms & Conditions shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of India. Any legal action or dispute arising out of or related to platform operations shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts situated in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India.
