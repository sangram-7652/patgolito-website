Data Safety & Transparent Deletion Process

In compliance with the Google Play Store User Data Policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, PATGOLITO provides a direct, accessible self-service pathway for customers and driver partners to permanently delete their account credentials, profiles, and associated personal information.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

Permanently Erased Data

  • User profile (Name, email address, phone number)
  • Saved delivery addresses and coordinate bookmarks
  • App login credentials, tokens, and active sessions
  • Marketing preferences and notification subscriptions
  • Driver vehicle records, photo documents, and KYC files

Legally Retained Records (Statutory)

  • Completed trip tax invoices (required for 7 years under Indian GST and Income Tax laws)
  • Banking and UPI transaction references for fraud prevention
  • Carriage logs under the Carriage by Road Act, 2007

How to Submit Account Deletion

You can submit an account deletion request using either of the following methods:

Method 1: Direct In-App & Web Request Form

Use our interactive web form on this page (renders above when JavaScript is enabled), select your registered mobile number, and receive an instant Tracking Request ID (`DEL-PAT-XXXXXX`).

Method 2: Email to Legal Compliance Team

Send an email to support@patgolito.com or official@patgolito.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Mobile Number]".

Method 3: Direct Helpline Coordination

Call our central Lucknow dispatch and support hotline at +91-9278499882 during working hours (9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST).

Processing Window: All verified deletion requests are executed within 7 business days. A confirmation SMS or email notification is dispatched upon completion.
