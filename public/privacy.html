PRIVACY POLICY – PATGOLITO

Operating Entity: Zoago Logistics Solutions Private Limited • Brand: PATGOLITO • Jurisdiction: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Patgolito (operated by Zoago Logistics Solutions Private Limited) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when users access our website, mobile services, or transportation platform. By using Patgolito services, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Patgolito may collect personal information including name, mobile number, email address, pickup and delivery locations, transaction details, and communication records. Such information may be collected when users create bookings, contact support, submit enquiries, or use our services. Information may also be collected where required for legal, operational, or compliance purposes.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

Information collected by Patgolito is used for processing bookings, coordinating transportation services, facilitating communication, improving operational efficiency, and enhancing user experience. Information may also be used for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and service improvement initiatives.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Patgolito may share information with drivers, vehicle owners, logistics partners, payment service providers, technology vendors, and government authorities where necessary. Such sharing is limited to legitimate business, operational, or legal requirements. Patgolito does not sell personal information to unrelated third parties.

4. Geolocation Data

Our transportation services rely on location tracking to calculate distances, suggest optimal pickup and drop points, match nearby drivers, and provide delivery tracking. Users may control location permissions through device settings, though disabling location access may limit platform features.

5. Data Storage and Security

Patgolito implements reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill service commitments, comply with statutory tax and accounting obligations (under GST and Carriage by Road laws), resolve disputes, and maintain operational integrity. Users may request account and data deletion at any time through our Account Deletion Portal.

7. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of, or updating of their personal information maintained by Patgolito. Requests may be submitted through official support channels subject to identity verification.
